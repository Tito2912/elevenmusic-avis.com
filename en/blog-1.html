Create Your First Music with ElevenMusic AI (Complete Guide)

Updated May 9, 2026 · Reading time: 10 min

ElevenMusic by ElevenLabs revolutionizes music creation by letting anyone generate original tracks in seconds — no instrument required, no DAW knowledge needed. You describe what you want in plain text and the AI composes for you. This step-by-step guide covers everything from account creation to final export.

What Is ElevenMusic, Exactly?

ElevenMusic is the music-generation module of ElevenLabs, the same company behind industry-leading AI voices. Launched in late 2024, it uses an audio diffusion model trained on a licensed music library to create original tracks from a simple text prompt.

What sets it apart from Suno or Udio:

  • Native integration inside the ElevenLabs ecosystem — one account for voice AND music.
  • Licensing agreements with rights holders (Merlin, Kobalt) to reduce commercial-claim risk.
  • API access for developers who want to automate music generation in their own apps.
  • Working free plan: 10 generations/month, no credit card required.

Step 1: Access ElevenMusic and Create an Account

Go to the official ElevenMusic website and create a free account. Sign-up takes under 2 minutes with an email or Google account. The Free plan includes 10 generations per month — plenty to explore the tool.

Tip: If you already have an ElevenLabs account for AI voices, it gives you direct access to ElevenMusic — no need to re-register.

Step 2: Understand the Interface in 3 Minutes

The ElevenMusic studio has 3 main areas:

  • Prompt area: The text field where you describe your music. This is where everything happens.
  • Advanced settings: Duration, musical style, suggested instruments, tempo (BPM), mood. These are optional but significantly improve output quality.
  • Player and history: Generations are saved automatically. You can replay, download, or re-generate with variations.

On first visit, explore ElevenLabs' built-in presets — they showcase the range of available genres (pop, cinematic, lo-fi, electro, jazz, world music, etc.).

Step 3: Writing a Good Music Prompt

Prompt quality is the single biggest factor in output quality. A vague prompt produces generic music; a precise prompt produces exactly what you imagined. Here is the formula that works consistently:

[Genre] + [Instruments] + [Mood/Atmosphere] + [Tempo] + [Use case] + [Optional duration]

Concrete examples by use case:

Step 5: Generate, Listen, Iterate

Click "Generate" — generation takes 15 to 45 seconds depending on requested duration. If the result doesn't fully satisfy you:

  1. Re-generate with the same prompt: each generation is unique, you'll get a different variation.
  2. Add details: if the rhythm is too slow, add "tempo 130 BPM"; if the melody is too busy, add "stripped-back arrangement".
  3. Segment for long tracks: generate intro, verse, and chorus separately, then assemble in an audio editor.

Step 6: Export and Use Your Music

Once satisfied, several export options are available:

  • MP3 or WAV download: available on all plans, including Free.
  • Direct share link: to send a preview to a client or collaborator.
  • API integration: for developers who want to automate music generation in their workflow.

For commercial use (monetized YouTube, ads, streaming), check your ElevenLabs plan's terms. Creator and higher plans include full commercial rights.
